# Adversarial audit — 2026-08-20

Three read-only audits by independent agents: the ingest/store layer, the
interfaces/platform/myteam layer, and a reachability trace over `sim/`, `eval/`
and `models/`. Every finding below carries `file:line`. Nothing here is
speculation unless it says INFERRED.

The audits were told to assume the project has a history of code that looked
finished but was not, and to go looking for more of it. They found more of it.

---

## The one that corrupts results

**Historical availability is fabricated for every player in four seasons.**

`fpl_edge/ingest/vaastav.py:589-594` deliberately refuses to invent data — the
per-gameweek archive carries no availability, so `status`, `chance_of_playing_next_round`
and `news` are written as NULL, with a comment saying that filling them with
`"a"` would fabricate the most decision-relevant field in the table. The
warehouse confirms it: `status`, `news` and `can_select` are **100% NULL across
all 109,956 historical `fact_player_state` rows** (2022-23 through 2025-26).

Two readers then fill exactly the gap the writer refused to fill:

- `fpl_edge/ingest/injuries.py:136` — `status = df["status"].fillna("a")`, so
  availability becomes "available" and `play_prob` falls through
  `STATUS_PRIOR["a"] = 1.00`. The minutes model receives a fabricated 1.0.
- `fpl_edge/store/warehouse.py:206` — `keep &= df["status"].isin(["a","d"]) | df["status"].isna()`,
  so **every injured, suspended and on-loan player in four seasons is
  `selectable()`**.

Any backtest over 2022-26 optimises a squad that can pick suspended players.
This silently invalidates historical evaluation, which is the one thing that
would tell us whether any of this works. Fix before any backtesting work.

## Correctness and data

1. **`read_copy` leaks temp copies of the database.**
   `fpl_edge/store/warehouse.py:379-402` copies the db to a `mkdtemp` and never
   registers cleanup; `close()` (`:566`) only closes the connection. **430
   orphaned `fpl-read-*` directories totalling 5.1 GB** were measured on this
   machine. `fpl_edge/platform/query.py:69-85` wraps it correctly with a
   contextmanager that names the problem in its docstring; every other caller
   leaks, including `TaskContext.read()` at `fpl_edge/jobs/deadline_dag.py:503`
   — i.e. every DAG tick. Fix belongs in `read_copy`, not per-caller.

2. **`dim_event` is empty for every historical season.** 152 rows, all 2026-27.
   `vaastav.ingest_season` (`:660-687`) computes the full deadline calendar via
   `build_calendar` (`:286`), uses it to stamp everything, then throws it away.
   Consequence: `Snapshot.deadline(season, gw)` and `Snapshot.next_gw()`
   (`warehouse.py:242-254`) raise `KeyError` for 2022-23 … 2025-26. **A
   backtest cannot ask the warehouse when any historical deadline was.**

3. **`fact_odds_derived` has 1,720 rows, real maths, and no reader.** Grepped
   repo-wide for the table and its market names: the only non-test hits are the
   writer and a passing mention in `platform/query.py:198`. Its module docstring
   (`ingest/odds_derived.py:6`) says "the projection model" consumes it. No such
   consumer exists. Worse, its `PIT_KEYS` registration is an **import-time side
   effect** (`odds_derived.py:145-147`), so `Snapshot.table("fact_odds_derived")`
   raises `KeyError` in any process that has not imported that module.

4. **The odds credit ledger is not crash-safe.** `ingest/odds_markets.py:313-317`
   records spend only on the success path; credits are spent before it. A
   mid-run failure under-counts the monthly cap on the next run.

5. **Provenance is recorded manually and mostly isn't.** `ingest/http.py`
   archives every body, but `record_fetch` is a separate call the caller must
   make. Measured: `data/raw/fpl_api/` holds **392 files**; `raw_fetch` holds
   **8 `fpl_api` rows**.

6. **`Fetched.from_cache` is hardcoded `False`** (`ingest/http.py:38,93`) and the
   docstring's caching claim is not implemented. `get_json` always hits network.

7. **Stale model cards assert the opposite of the data.**
   `models/team_goals/market.py:150` says "SYNTHETIC DATA ONLY. fact_odds is
   empty"; `dixon_coles.py:390` says the market comparison is "UNMEASURED on real
   data". `fact_odds` holds **115,025 rows**. The market baseline is measurable
   and these cards say it isn't.

**What is genuinely strong** and should not be disturbed: the point-in-time
mechanism. `PIT_KEYS` + `Snapshot.table()`'s `ROW_NUMBER() … WHERE as_of <= ?`
is correct; `Snapshot.warehouse` raises `LeakageError` and the escape hatch
demands a ≥20-char reason (only three production uses); `append()` is the sole
write path, append-only, with tz-awareness checked *before* `pd.to_datetime`
(which would otherwise silently localise), fixture-finished-before-kickoff
guards, and `ConflictingFactError` on contradictory replays. Verified: no
`UPDATE`/`DELETE` against any fact table in production code, and
`fact_player_fixture.as_of < fact_fixture.kickoff_utc` returns zero rows across
four seasons. Timestamps are semantically differentiated per fact type —
points finalise at 09:00 UK the day after the last match, fixtures get two rows
(schedule at deadline, result at kickoff+2h), prices stamp at the deadline.
Player identity resolution is 100% across all four seasons with zero conflicts.

## The UI is largely broken, and the breakage is invisible

The bundle was never opened in a browser. There is no frontend test of any kind.

1. **The price radar panel can never display data.** `panels.py:75-83` pins it to
   `layout="table"`; `renderTable` (`web/dist/index.html:145`) reads `res.rows`
   and returns an empty box when absent. The panel's result schema
   (`platform/scripts/prices.py:73-94`) is `additionalProperties: false` with no
   `rows` property at all — a `rows` key would fail validation. So the panel
   renders "No data." **even when the backend returns a full set of risers and
   fallers.** Real data, silently discarded, with a message that blames the data.

2. **Every player on the squad pitch renders `£NaN`.** `index.html:185,196` read
   `p.price_tenths`; `squad_overview` emits `price` in £m
   (`platform/scripts/squad.py:180`) and its schema forbids `price_tenths`.

3. **The fixture difficulty colouring is unreachable and its caption is a
   standing lie.** `index.html:222` reads `f.opponents[0].difficulty`, which the
   schema (`fixtures.py:69-79`) forbids. The caption at `:233-234` tells the
   reader "Colour is our own fitted difficulty where available" — describing a
   capability with no code path.

4. **Idea outcome chips never colour.** JS tests `"correct"`/`"incorrect"`
   (`index.html:244`); the panel's vocabulary is `hit`/`miss`
   (`scripts/ideas.py:243-247`). The CLI uses a *third* vocabulary
   (`cli/main.py:266`).

5. **Sorting a table deletes its provenance line.** `index.html:154` clears
   `host` on a header click; the provenance footer was appended to that host
   (`:272`). One click removes the thing the registry exists to guarantee.

6. **The deadline countdown is hardcoded.** `index.html:110` —
   `new Date("2026-08-21T17:30:00Z")`, never fetched from `dim_event`, with no
   API route that would supply it. From GW2 it reads "deadline passed Nh" forever.

7. **Latent repeat of the same trap:** `Panel.layout` is an unvalidated string
   and `index.html:270` falls back to `renderTable`, so any future panel with a
   new layout renders as "Nothing to show yet" rather than erroring.

Credit where due: there are no empty `catch{}` blocks; API errors surface.
The silence is structural — shape mismatches producing empty renders.

## Report sections vanish silently

`fpl_edge/interfaces/squad_section.py:25-26` returns `None` when the plan file
is missing, with the comment *"declared as a gap by the report's
missing-section list"*. **That comment is false.** `missing()`
(`interfaces/report.py:75-77`) computes `EXPECTED − registered()`, and the
section registers `provides="squad"` unconditionally at import, so `"squad"` is
never a gap. `weekly_report` (`report.py:197`) drops a `None` with no warning.

Net effect: **with no `gw1_plan.json`, `fpl weekly` prints neither the
recommended squad nor any statement that it is missing.** Compounding it,
`PLAN_PATH` (`:19`) is a *relative* path while every other artefact lookup
anchors to the repo root — so running from any other directory makes the
section disappear.

Same mechanism hits `_tracking_section` (`report.py:122-126`) and the theses
section, whose `provides="theses"` isn't even a key in `EXPECTED`, so it can
never be reported as a gap under any circumstance.

**The fix already exists elsewhere in the codebase.** `interfaces/dossier.py:101-107`
raises if a section has neither body nor gap, and iterates `EXPECTED` rather
than a registry, so a section cannot be absent. The report layer should adopt
the dossier's contract.

## Auth, and surfaces that disagree

1. **The 401-retry path bypasses the cross-process refresh lock.**
   `myteam/private.py:155` calls `manager._refresh(manager._read())` directly.
   Every other path takes both locks (`tokens.py:201`, `:225`). This is the path
   that fires exactly when tokens are contended and rotating — the scenario the
   flock was added for, documented at `tokens.py:38-43`.

2. **`TokenManager`'s advertised thread-safety is not real.** `_lock` is a
   per-instance `threading.Lock` (`tokens.py:112-113`) and every call site
   constructs a fresh `TokenManager()` (`private.py:146`, `cli.py:98-100`,
   `report.py:118`, `cli.py:558`). Only the flock serialises anything.

3. **Telegram `/myteam` omits `private=`.** `myteam/bot.py:220-228` calls
   `reconstruct(...)` with no private client, while the CLI, the weekly report
   and the dashboard all pass one. So pre-deadline with a working token, the bot
   says the squad is unknown while the dashboard shows it.
   `platform/scripts/squad.py:3-8` calls a panel and a Telegram answer
   disagreeing about your team "the single worst failure this platform can
   have" — and this is that failure.

4. **A dead session degrades `fpl weekly` silently.** `myteam/report.py:121-126`
   catches `StaleSessionError` with a comment claiming it is "reported in the
   section body via provenance". It is not. `qa.py:169-176` — the path the
   dashboard panel uses — swallows every exception with no log at all.

5. **`fpl myteam whynot` sends the user to configure the auth path that
   provably returns 403.** `cli.py:468-471` lists `FPL_SESSION_COOKIE` as source
   #1; `tokens.py:32-37` records the measured fact that a cookie alone now
   returns 403. `myteam/__init__.py:4-9` still claims the engine "will not store
   a session cookie".

6. **`make deploy-platform` is documented (`MVP.md:16-20`) and does not exist.**
   Worse: `deploy/com.fpledge.platform.plist` **does** exist and nothing in the
   repo installs it. Same defect class as the missing `auth` command.

## Dead code: the scale of it

A reachability trace of the import closure from every production entrypoint
(`cli/`, `jobs/`, `interfaces/`, `oracle/`, `myteam/`, `opt/`, `rank/`,
`platform/`, `scripts/`, `Makefile`) found **14 modules with zero production
reach**:

`sim/experiments.py` (13 of 16 symbols referenced nowhere at all),
`sim/calibration.py`, `sim/live.py`, `sim/synthetic.py`, `sim/utility.py`,
`models/minutes/{baselines,dataset,evaluate,hierarchical}.py`,
`models/team_goals/{baselines,blend,evaluate,metrics,synthetic}.py`.

Two findings matter more than the list:

- **`SeasonSimulator` is never instantiated in production.** Only
  `sim/experiments.py:102` and tests construct it. The production path into
  `sim/` runs through `_paired_validation` (`myteam/recommend.py:805`), whose
  `validator` argument **is never supplied by any caller**. The entire `sim`
  package is reachable-but-never-executed.
- **`strategies/` and `models/ensemble/` are wholly orphaned packages** —
  nothing outside them imports either. `models/field/` is likewise outside the
  production closure, despite being the largest consumer of `sim/field.py`.

`sim/utility.py`'s `rank_utility` is test-only; the `rank_utility=` kwargs in
`opt/` and `config.py` are unrelated parameter names, not that function.

So the rank-aware machinery the project is *about* is, today, not wired into
anything a user can run.

## What this means

The engine's foundations — point-in-time correctness, append-only history,
identity resolution, honest-gap patterns in `dossier.py` and `myteam/state.py`
— are genuinely good and better than most of what this class of tool ships.

What is missing is not sophistication. It is **connection**: the simulator is
not called, the rank objective is not reachable, the derived odds have no
reader, the projections are not blended, the UI discards three panels' worth of
real data, the report drops sections without saying so, and the historical
availability that every backtest depends on is fabricated at read time.

The highest-value work is almost entirely wiring and honesty, not new models.
